How the formula works: 134 pairs have a fixed recipe, and one pair is gender-dependent (Katress/Wixen). Otherwise the child is the species whose CombiRank is closest to ⌊(rankA + rankB + 1) / 2⌋ among the 183 species in the generic pool — variants and legendaries that only exist as recipe children can never come out of the formula. On an exact tie, the higher CombiRank wins. The 2 steps that depend on the tie-break are flagged, with safe alternatives.

Gender: every step needs a ♂ of one parent and a ♀ of the other (which one is which doesn't matter — except Katress/Wixen). Offspring are ~50/50, so intermediates that get reused should be kept in both genders. Gender can be swapped with the Pal Reverser.

Steps with a green edge are ready now — both parents exist among the pals you own (Paldex tab) or have bred (checked steps). Where gender is locked (Katress/Wixen only) the card shows ♀/♂ pins on the parents and a padlock — otherwise the split is free, as long as it's one male and one female. Everything in the same phase can run in parallel across multiple farms.

Hatchery operations

The Breeding Farm is tech 19, the standard Cake tech 17 — everything in this plan runs on standard cakes. Incubation time was halved in 1.0 (new worlds), and the Ancient Hatchery (lvl 76) waits in the endgame.

Standard cake recipe: 5 Flour · 8 Red Berries · 7 Milk · 8 Eggs · 2 Honey (Mozzarina, Chikipi and Beegarde on a Ranch cover milk/eggs/honey). The species plan only needs standard cakes — save the expensive ones for passive/IV/mutation hunting afterwards.

The order for one perfect pal

1) Species (this plan) → 2) passives (Pal Surgery Table, tech 38: implants for 10–50k gold per operation; standard implants are not consumed — chain-breeding for passives is effectively obsolete) → 3) IVs (Mushroom Cake, same-species breeding; inheritance per stat: 30 % father / 30 % mother / 40 % random) → 4) condensing → 5) Awakening last. Gender is swapped with the Pal Reverser (consumed; bought with Bounty Tokens and more).

Condensing (1.0)

4★ costs 48 copies total: 4 / 8 / 12 / 24 per star (old guides say 116 — outdated). Each star raises one work suitability a level; 4★ raises all of them, and partner skill level = stars + 1. Mutant eggs hatch at 2★ — leaving only 12 + 24 = 36. Tip: Starfruit (bought with Dog Coins) can substitute for copies in the condenser.

Mutation

~1% per egg with a standard cake (~3% with Extravagant). The mutant hatches as an Alpha with 2★, IVs ~91–100 and four passives of which at least two are rainbow — but the species can differ from (and outrank) the pair's normal child. Volume wins: run several farms, and let Braloha (+20–50% egg speed) and Dynamoff (−20–40% incubation time) work for you once they're bred in Phase 1.
